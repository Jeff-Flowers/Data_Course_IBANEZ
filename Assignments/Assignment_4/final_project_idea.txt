Final Project Idea - Jeffrey Ibanez

Basically I want to do a project about opsins, the photoreceptive proteins that enable vision.
More specifically I intend to analyze the genomic and proteomic data of opsins across a variety
of species and find correlations between the differences in sequence/structure and the environment
the organism is suited for. Maybe I'll look into novel opsin design with alphafold. Maybe.
I expect the differences I find will be dependant on the uses an organism has for vision.
Sea creatures will have opsins optimised for the absorption spectrum of water, predators will
have opsins optimised for seeing their prey, things like that.

I'll certainly be using genetic data that encodes opsins, and comparing them, which I will
source from NCBI. I will be doing BLASTS of both the dna sequences and the proteins.
The data will look like text listing the genetic sequences, possibly with confidence intervals
attatched. Protein files will be PDBs, and look like text that must be converted into a 3D model.
